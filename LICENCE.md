## 🧩 About

**ARK Survival Loot Overlay** is a comprehensive mod menu for ARK: Survival Evolved and ARK 2 featuring resource ESP, spawn map overlay, taming assistant, base scanner, loot tracker, and auto-collector.

Based on popular mods like **ARK Smart Breeding**, **Dino Scanner**, and **Loot Helper**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Overlays
- Resource ESP — highlight metal, crystal, obsidian, oil, and rich nodes
- Spawn Map Overlay — display creature spawn points and supply drop locations
- Loot Tracker — log and filter loot drops by quality and item
- Base Scanner — detect enemy structures, turrets, and vaults
- Player ESP — show nearby players and tribes

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- Skin Unlocker — unlock all cosmetic skins and chibi pets
- Color Picker — apply any dye color to gear and dinos
- Emote Unlock — access all emotes without achievements

### 🏋️ Utility & Automation
- Taming Assistant — calculate taming progress, food, and narcotics
- Auto-Collector — automatically harvest nearby resources
- Instant Craft — craft items without queue time
- Weight Reduction — increase carry weight temporarily
